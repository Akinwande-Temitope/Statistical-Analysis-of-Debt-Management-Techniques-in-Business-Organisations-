# Statistical-Analysis-of-Debt-Management-Techniques-in-Business-Organisations-
This project examines debt management techniques in business organizations, using Dangote Industries Limited as a case study.  Debt financing plays an important role in business operations, capital investment, and organizational expansion. 
However, effective debt management is essential to maintaining financial stability, improving profitability, and ensuring long-term business sustainability.

In Nigeria, businesses operate within a challenging economic environment characterized by fluctuating interest rates, inflation, exchange rate volatility, and other economic uncertainties. These factors make effective debt management an important aspect of corporate financial decision-making.

This study statistically examines the debt structure and financial performance of Dangote Industries Limited over a 15-year period (2010–2024), using published financial statements and secondary data.

Research Objectives

The specific objectives of this study are to:

1. Identify the common sources of debt financing used by Nigerian businesses.
2. Examine the debt management techniques adopted by business organizations.
3. Assess the effectiveness of these techniques.
4. Evaluate the challenges affecting debt management practices.
5. Recommend strategies for improving debt management practices.
